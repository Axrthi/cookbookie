Egg & Cheese Toast

Ingredients:

  • 1 slice of bread
  • 1 egg
  • 1 slice of Cheese
  • 1 tsp butter
  • salt & pepper

Instructions:

  1. Butter a hot pan.
  2. Cook the egg until firm.
  3. Place bread in the pan, add the egg on top.
  4. Add cheese and cover.
  5. Cook 1-2 mins until cheese melts.
  6. Sprinkle salt & pepper. Serve hot!
