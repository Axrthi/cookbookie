Cheesy Garlic Bread

Ingredients:

  • 1 bread
  • 2 tbsp butter (softened)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder (or 1 clove minced garlic)
  • ½ cup shredded cheese (mozzarella or any cheese you like)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix butter and garlic, then spread it on the bread.
  3. Sprinkle cheese on top.
  4. Bake for 10 minutes (or until cheese melts).
  5. Enjoy!
