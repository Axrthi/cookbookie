Fruit Punch

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups orange juice
  • 2 cups pineapple juice
  • 2 cups mixed fruit juice (grape, apple, or mango)
  • 1 cup soda (Sprite/7UP) or sparkling water (optional)
  • ½ cup lemon juice (for a tangy kick)
  • 2 tbsp sugar or honey (optional)
  • 1 cup chopped fruits (strawberries, oranges, pineapples, etc.)
  • Ice cubes

Instructions:

  1. Mix all juices in a large jug.
  2. Stir in sugar or honey (if using).
  3. Add soda/sparkling water for fizz.
  4. Add soda/sparkling water for fizz.
  5. Serve chilled & enjoy!
